# Using your Sonello phone number

Last updated: 2026-07-04

When your AI receptionist is set up, you get a dedicated **Sonello phone number**. This guide explains the ways you can route calls to and from it, with step-by-step setup for each scenario.

## TL;DR

There are two common ways to use your Sonello number:

1. **Sonello answers everything** — publish your Sonello number (or forward your main line to it) so the AI receptionist picks up every call.
2. **You answer first, Sonello backs you up** — let your own phone ring first, and forward *unanswered* calls to Sonello.

Sonello can also **transfer or escalate** a live call to another number (you, a manager, or a specific department) based on rules you set in your portal.

---

## The three routing options

| Option | Who answers first | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Sonello is your main line** | Sonello (the AI) | New numbers, or making Sonello the front door for all calls |
| **B. Overflow / no-answer** | You, then Sonello | Keeping your existing number and using Sonello as backup |
| **C. Sonello forwards to you** | Sonello, then a human | Escalating urgent or complex calls to a person |

You can combine **C** with either **A** or **B** — Sonello always follows your escalation rules once it's on the call.

## Option A — Make Sonello your main line

Use this when you want the AI receptionist to answer **every** call.

You have two ways to do it:

### A1. Give out your Sonello number directly

1. Use your Sonello number on your website, Google Business Profile, business cards, and ads.
2. Every caller reaches Sonello first.
3. Sonello answers, qualifies the caller, books appointments, and escalates to you when your rules say so.

No phone settings to change — just publish the number.

### A2. Forward your existing number to Sonello (unconditional)

Keep advertising your current business number, but send **all** calls to Sonello:

1. Find your Sonello number in **E.164** format, e.g. `+131****0123`.
2. On your business line, set up **unconditional call forwarding** to your Sonello number.
3. Make a test call to your business number — Sonello should answer.

**Example (most carriers):** dial `*72` + your Sonello number to enable, `*73` to cancel. Codes vary by carrier — check your carrier portal if unsure.

---

## Option B — You answer first, Sonello catches the rest

Use this when you want to keep answering your own calls but never miss one. Your phone rings first; if you don't pick up, the call rolls to Sonello.

1. Get your Sonello number in E.164 format, e.g. `+131****0123`.
2. On your phone or carrier portal, set up **conditional forwarding** for **No answer** (and optionally **Busy** / **Unreachable**).
3. Set the ring time before forwarding to **15–30 seconds** (about 20 is a good default).
4. Point the forward destination at your Sonello number.
5. Make 3 test calls from different phones and let each ring through.

### Setup by device / provider

**iPhone** — iPhone settings only support *unconditional* forwarding. For **No answer** forwarding, use your carrier portal or carrier support.

**Android** — Open **Phone → Settings → Call forwarding → Forward when unanswered**, then enter your Sonello number.

**AT&T** — **MyAT&T → Manage devices → Call forwarding settings** → add a **No answer** rule pointing to your Sonello number.

**Verizon** — **My Verizon → Devices → your phone → Call forwarding** → add **No Answer** forwarding to your Sonello number.

**Google Voice / Google Fi** — Add your Sonello number as a forwarding destination; check screening and voicemail settings, then test.

**VoIP / PBX / business phone system** — Look for **Call Handling**, **Call Routing**, or **No answer forwarding**. Ring the extension first, then route **NOANSWER** to your Sonello number. Start with a 20-second timeout.

> **TIP** Set your ring time a little shorter than your voicemail pickup time, so Sonello grabs the call *before* voicemail does.

## Option C — Have Sonello forward calls to another number

Sonello can hand a live call off to a real person — you, a manager, or a specific line — when a caller needs it. This is controlled entirely from your **client portal**, no phone settings required.

### How to set it up

1. Log in to your **Sonello client portal**.
2. Open **Agent Settings**.
3. Set the **Escalation phone number** — the number Sonello should transfer to (E.164 format, e.g. `+131****0199`).
4. Add **Escalation rules** in plain English describing *when* Sonello should transfer. For example:
   - "Transfer to me if the caller is an existing customer with an urgent issue."
   - "If someone asks to speak to a person, connect them to (817) 555‑0199."
   - "Forward billing questions to the office manager."
5. Save. Changes take effect on the next call.

### What the caller experiences

1. Sonello answers and talks with the caller as usual.
2. When a rule is met, Sonello says something like *"Let me connect you with the team directly,"* and transfers the call to your escalation number.
3. If the transfer number doesn't answer, Sonello can take a message or offer to book a callback.

> **NOTE** Escalation rules are guidance for the AI, written in plain language — the more specific you are about *who* and *when*, the more reliably Sonello routes the call.

## Putting it together — example setups

**A solo owner who wants full coverage**
Option **B** (ring my cell first, forward no-answer to Sonello) + Option **C** (Sonello escalates urgent existing-customer calls back to my cell). You catch the calls you can; Sonello covers the rest and only interrupts you for the important ones.

**A business making Sonello the front desk**
Option **A2** (forward the main business line to Sonello) + Option **C** (Sonello transfers to the right department based on escalation rules). Every call is answered instantly, and humans only get the calls that truly need them.

**A brand-new number**
Option **A1** (advertise the Sonello number directly). Simplest possible setup — nothing to forward.

## Test checklist

Before you rely on any setup, run these tests:

1. Call from your own phone.
2. Call from a second cell phone.
3. Call from a landline if possible.
4. For Option B, let it ring until forwarding kicks in.
5. Confirm each time:
   - Sonello answers (or the human does, for escalations)
   - Caller ID looks right
   - The call shows up in your portal call log

## Troubleshooting

- **Sonello never answers** → double-check the Sonello number and that forwarding is enabled.
- **Calls forward too fast** → increase the ring time before forwarding.
- **Escalation doesn't transfer** → confirm the escalation phone number is saved in your portal and your rules describe the situation clearly.
- **Caller ID changes** → some carriers rewrite caller ID on forwarded calls; this is normal.
- **Looping calls** → make sure you're only forwarding your line *to* Sonello, not Sonello back to a line that forwards to Sonello.
