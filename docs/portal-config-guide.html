SMS Campaigns

Bulk-text your leads with follow-up messages, promotions, and booking links. Opt-outs are honored automatically.

  1. Open the SMS Services tab in your portal sidebar.
  2. Name your campaign — e.g. "Spring Follow-up".
  3. Set the throttle — messages per minute (default 30). Lower this if your carrier requires it.
  4. Write your message template — use {{name}} for personalization. Example: Hi {{name}}! We have a 10% spring special. Reply to book or call us at {{business_phone}}.
  5. Upload your lead list — one per line: phone, name. Phone is required; name is optional. Max 200 leads per campaign.
  6. Click "Create SMS Campaign" — messages begin sending immediately within your throttle limit.
  7. Monitor progress — click any campaign in the list to see per-lead delivery status (pending, sent, delivered, failed, opted-out).

Opt-out handling: leads who reply STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, or END are automatically excluded from all future campaigns. They can opt back in by replying START.

AI Callback Flows

Your AI agent can place outbound calls to leads automatically — from inbound lead follow-up to missed-call callbacks and bulk campaigns.

A. Inbound Lead → Auto Call

  1. A lead submits your web form (Lead Intake endpoint).
  2. The AI instantly sends a follow-up SMS (no business-hours check).
  3. If within calling hours, the AI places an outbound call to the lead.
  4. If outside calling hours, the call is skipped — the lead still gets the SMS.

Configured automatically when you set up Lead Intake on the Integrations tab. No extra toggle needed.

B. Missed Call → Auto Callback

  1. A caller dials your Auzora number and the call is missed (no-answer, busy, or failed).
  2. The AI sends a follow-up SMS to the caller immediately.
  3. If auto-callback is enabled, the AI waits a short delay (default 2 min), then calls the caller back — but only within business hours.
  4. If outside business hours, the callback is rescheduled for the next hour and retried.

Contact support to enable auto-callback for your account. Rate-limited to one callback per caller per hour.

C. Bulk Call Campaigns

  1. Open the Outbound tab in your portal sidebar.
  2. Name your campaign — e.g. "March Re-engagement".
  3. Add script context (optional) — e.g. "Mention 10% spring discount for new customers". The AI weaves this into its natural conversation.
  4. Set retries — max retry attempts after the first call (default 2 = 3 total attempts per lead).
  5. Set retry delay — minutes between attempts (default 60, min 15).
  6. Upload your lead list — one per line: phone, name, service, notes. Phone is required; other fields optional. Max 200 leads.
  7. Click "Create Campaign" — the AI starts dialing within your business hours.
  8. Monitor progress — click any campaign to see per-lead call outcomes (reached, no-answer, voicemail, opted-out).

Calls only happen within your configured business hours (Scheduling tab). Leads who say "stop calling" or "don't call" are automatically opted out.

D. Manual Single Call

  1. Open the Outbound tab.
  2. Enter a phone number in the "Quick Call" box at the top.
  3. Click "Place Call" — the AI dials the lead immediately (within business hours).

Business Hours

All outbound calls (campaigns, callbacks, lead follow-up) respect your business hours configured on the Scheduling tab. SMS messages send regardless of business hours.
